# Privacy Policy - Job Summary Extractor

**Effective Date:** June 6, 2026

## Overview

Job Summary Extractor is a Chrome extension that helps job seekers quickly extract key information from job postings. **This extension collects absolutely no data.**

## Data Collection - None

This extension does NOT collect, store, or transmit ANY personal information whatsoever.

## What This Extension Does

When you click the extension icon on a job posting page:

1. Reads the job description text on the current page only
2. Extracts key information (company, experience, location, salary, recruiter contact)
3. Displays this information in a popup window
4. Copies the summary to your clipboard only when you click "Copy"

## What This Extension Does NOT Do

- ❌ No data collection of any kind
- ❌ No tracking of browsing history
- ❌ No external server requests or API calls
- ❌ No analytics, tracking pixels, or cookies
- ❌ No local storage of your information
- ❌ No background processes or automatic activation
- ❌ No modification of website content
- ❌ No sharing of information with third parties

## How Your Data Is Handled

**All operations happen entirely on your local device.**

- The extension only reads the specific job page you are viewing
- Only when you manually click the extension icon
- Never sends any data over the internet
- Never stores any data after you close the popup

## Permissions Explained

The extension uses minimal permissions for specific purposes:

| Permission | Why It's Needed | When It's Used |
|------------|-----------------|----------------|
| `activeTab` | Read job page content | Only when you click the icon |
| Host permissions | Access job sites (LinkedIn, Indeed, Glassdoor, etc.) | Only on job posting pages |

## Third-Party Access

This extension does not use any third-party services, APIs, or libraries that collect data. All code runs locally within your browser.

## Children's Privacy

This extension does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. It is designed for general job-seeking audiences.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated date will be reflected above. Users will be notified through the Chrome Web Store listing.
